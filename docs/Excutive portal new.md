# Unified Executive & Business Intelligence Portal Architecture

> **Version:** 2.0
> **Portal:** Executive & Business Intelligence (EBI)
> **Architecture Style:** Unified Hotel ERP Portal
> **Purpose:** Enterprise Decision Support, Executive Control, Analytics, KPI Management, AI Insights, and Strategic Planning

---

# 1. Overview

The **Executive & Business Intelligence (EBI) Portal** is the executive command center of the Hotel ERP. Unlike operational portals that create and manage transactions, this portal **does not own operational data**. Instead, it consolidates information from every ERP module into a single source of truth for strategic decision-making.

It provides executives, owners, general managers, regional directors, and corporate leadership with real-time visibility into hotel performance through dashboards, KPIs, scorecards, AI insights, forecasting, benchmarking, and enterprise analytics.

The portal supports both **single-property** and **multi-property hotel groups**, enabling consolidated reporting across brands, regions, and business units.

# 28. Ownership Boundaries

## Owned by Executive & Business Intelligence

* Enterprise Dashboards
* KPI Management
* Enterprise Analytics
* Executive Reporting
* AI Insights
* Forecasting
* Benchmarking
* Strategic Planning Support
* Enterprise Scorecards
* Cross-Department Analytics

## Not Owned

This portal **does not own or edit operational transactions**.

All transactional data remains owned by its originating portal.

The Executive Portal consumes, aggregates, analyzes, and visualizes enterprise data without becoming the system of record.
