# Unified Human Resources & Payroll Portal Architecture

> **Version:** 1.0
> **Portal:** Human Resources & Payroll (HRMS)
> **Architecture Style:** Unified Hotel ERP Portal

---

# 1. Overview

The **Human Resources & Payroll Portal (HRMS)** is the workforce management hub of the Hotel ERP. It manages the complete employee lifecycle—from recruitment and onboarding through attendance, scheduling, payroll, performance, learning, benefits, compliance, and offboarding.

The portal integrates with every operational department while maintaining ownership of all employee, organizational, and payroll information.
