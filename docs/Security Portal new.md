# Unified Security & Risk Portal Architecture

> **Version:** 1.0
> **Portal:** Security & Risk Management
> **Architecture Style:** Unified Hotel ERP Portal

---

# 1. Overview

The **Security & Risk Portal** is responsible for protecting guests, employees, visitors, assets, information, and hotel operations. It centralizes physical security, incident management, investigations, access control, emergency response, compliance, risk management, business continuity, and safety monitoring.

This portal integrates with the Front Office (PMS), Human Resources, Engineering, Housekeeping, Finance, Procurement, IT, and System Administration while maintaining ownership of all security operations and enterprise risk management.

# 29. Design Principles

* Centralized security operations
* Enterprise risk management
* Incident-driven workflows
* Real-time monitoring and alerting
* Mobile security officer support
* QR/NFC-enabled patrol verification
* Electronic access control integration
* CCTV integration with evidence management
* Business continuity and disaster recovery readiness
* Role-based access control (RBAC)
* Complete audit trail
* Multi-property support
* Multi-language support
* API-first integration
* Cloud-native architecture
* Scalable for hotel groups and resorts
